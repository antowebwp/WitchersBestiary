# Stage 22A - Unicorn companion

Based on the supplied concept: horse proportions, central segmented forward horn, violet eyes, pearl coat, layered pastel mane and long tail. The model, 256-pixel atlas and animation clips are generated by `tools/generate-unicorn.py`. Native front, side, rear and three-quarter views were compared with the concept. Render review prompted thicker exterior locks, a corrected horn angle, adjusted pixel colors and a saddle attachment aligned with the model. Minecraft directional lighting and the simpler game mesh differ from the concept illustration. Local images and a copy of this report are in `build/stage22a-review/`.

Unicorns spawn very rarely in flower forests, meadows and forests. Use a golden apple or golden carrot to bond. The apple guarantees bonding; each carrot has a one-in-three chance. Sneak-interact with an empty hand to toggle wait/follow. Vanilla saddle, movement and jump controls are available to the owner. Follow uses normal pathfinding in the loaded world.

In combat, the owner can recover 2.5 HP within 12 blocks and line of sight, with a persistent ten-second player cooldown shared by all unicorns. The horn briefly glows during the healing gesture. Lethal damage to a tamed unicorn starts 30 seconds of recovery on the same entity, dismounts the rider and suspends healing. Ownership, saddle, waiting and recovery survive saving.

- Targeted GameTests passed 3/3 including the Patchouli smoke test (`build/stage22a-seat.log`). They covered taming, owner-only mounting and jump availability, recovery and NBT, line of sight and shared healing cooldown. The first run revealed a creative mock consumption assumption; native Survival verified actual apple consumption.
- Two native clients verified owner-only riding, movement inputs, lethal recovery and same-entity dedicated restart (`build/stage22a-native.log`, run `120421`).
- Final mesh and saddle views are from run `121858`. Run `122321` confirmed different rendered front-leg phases during observed motion and captured the healing effect. Server displacement drives the tracked gait because remote vehicle velocity alone was insufficient. Dedicated save and shutdown passed. Final build and 13 mixin checks passed (`build/stage22a-gait-build.log`).
