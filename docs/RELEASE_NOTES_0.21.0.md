# Witcher's Bestiary 0.21.0

For Minecraft 1.21.1 and Java 21 on Fabric. Requires Fabric Loader 0.17 or later, Fabric API, Patchouli 1.21.1-93-FABRIC, and GeckoLib 4.8.4 or later in the 4.x series. Install the same mod version on the server and clients.

- Earn crowns from contracts and store them in a wallet. Trade with village and wandering Arcane Merchants, including rare materials and three premium swords.
- Brew 17 alchemy recipes and use category-specific oils and elixirs. Four sword enchantments add further combat options.
- Learn Igni, Aard, Quen, Yrden and Axii from the Bestiary. The separate Grimoire teaches Icy Grasp.
- Fight NPCs and the Rock Troll with nonfatal stamina combat. Challenge players through the Duel Stone or sneak-interact with an Arcane Merchant to enter 3-, 4- or 5-round tournaments with personal titles and prizes.
- Track two clues and hunt contract targets up to 350 blocks from the notice board, outside their natural biomes when the terrain permits. Cancel active hunts through the board UI.
- Legendary offers have their own saved rotation. Newly accepted contracts pay by monster danger. Leshens and Fiends start at Epic, and the two legendary hunts pay 1,800 and 2,200 crowns. Previously accepted contracts retain their saved rewards.

Skill Tree and Spell Power integrations are optional. Tournament prizes retain a per-player, per-category cooldown. The final hotfix was tested with focused checks following the RC1 review.
